# Educational Psychedelic Awareness Center

Open educational research project helping families understand psychedelic safety, mental health studies, and open communication.

We provide evidence-based resources for parents, educators, and families to support safe and informed conversations.

## 📚 Research Topics
- Psychedelic safety and mental health research 2024-2026
- Family communication and harm reduction
- Educational guides for parents
